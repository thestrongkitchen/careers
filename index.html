Lead Prep Cook

$25/hr  ·  Wed–Sat, four 8-hour days  ·  No nights

No kitchen experience required. Leadership qualities a must.

The job

You own the hands-on side of our weekly production: receiving, prep and cooking, working from the prep lists and menu set by the Kitchen Manager. Each day you balance that day's tasks with getting ready for what needs to happen tomorrow.

Pay
$25/hr
Schedule
Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday. Four 8-hour days, no nights.
Where
109 Sanford St, Building 2, Hamden CT
Training
90-day structured training

Who we're looking for

Someone who wants to be part of the kitchen team, not someone who puts their head down, gets through the list and leaves.

  • Independent. You can take the prep list and run it on your own, while checking in and working with the team.
  • Good judgment. You make sound calls in the moment instead of following steps into a problem.
  • Speaks up. When something's unclear, you ask instead of guessing.
  • A real team player who communicates well and enjoys the people around them.

A week in the kitchen

Wed
Receive the week's order. Trim and marinate proteins, prep sauces and spice mixes, cut vegetables.
Thu
Start the cook cycle: pasta, ground proteins, rices, sauces, and cooling cooked food safely.
Fri
Potatoes, pasta mixes, pack rices, portion dressings and sides.
Sat
Finish the cook items, assemble burritos, close out the week.
