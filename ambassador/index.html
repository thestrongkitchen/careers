Who we're looking for

Follower count matters less than you'd think. We care more that you post regularly and that people trust what you say.

  • You already share your meals, workouts or routine online
  • Real over polished. iPhone photos of your real life are ideal.
  • You post consistently and reliably
  • You have a career or a family that prepared meals can help with

What you need

The application checks these first.

  • 18 or older
  • A public Instagram account you post on
  • You live where we deliver (New Haven County, Hartford County or most of Fairfield County) or can pick up in Hamden
  • One post a week for 4 weeks, with you in the picture
  • You're comfortable sharing a little of your own life in those posts
